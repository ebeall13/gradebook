What is the general purpose of your program? (1-2 sentences)
The General purpose of this program is to keep track of and organize the users grades and allow them to know their averages,
GPA, and other things they may not be able to do just by looking at the assignment numbers. 

Who are 2 possible expected users of your program? How does your program target them?
One possible expected user of the program is students. This program targets students by giving them a place to keep track of their various grades
organized by class, calculate their gpa (which they may need for academic related things), and find out their subjects in need of work.
The other possible expected user is a teacher. A teacher could use this program to keep track of specific students, allowing them to see all
their academic information in a easy place. My program targets them by allowing for teachers to add grades or subjects. 

Consider all features of your program. Name one non-negotiable of your project and one feature that is nice-to-have. Why do you feel this is the case?
One non-negotiable of the program is the method that finds the average grade. This essential because it fuels all the other methods as a helper method. 
One feature that is nice to have but not essentail is the one that tells you your worst and best class. This isn't esstential because the program already 
gives you your average in every class, so you could just look at that and see which one is your best.

What are two improvements or additional features that could be included in future iterations of your project? What value would they add?
One additional feature I think would be cool is a way to weight your grades. In normal high school, grades are generally so all worth the same thing,
so it would add value to my program by making it more realistic and versatile.
Another feature I would add is a teacher option, where the teacher can go in and edit grades or change them. I think this would add value to my program 
by giving it more use and making it less prone to error (ex, a grade is added wrong)
